# Bland Quest Auto: the tree of tasks

How one simple job turns into many. Every task opens more tasks. Some branches end for good, some loop back, and a few actually move you forward. Each task says **where** in the game you do it, so the tree links every screen and every place on the city map.

## How it all hangs together

**You always have something to try, but never know if it's the right thing.** The notebook is what joins every screen up.

- **The notebook** (in your pocket on every screen)
  - Holds the jobs you know about, as a tree: the big job at the top, the smaller jobs it has turned into below
  - Each job shows what you've tried: ✔ worked, ✘ dead end (crossed out), ○ still open
  - Each open job lists its **leads**: things to try, each saying where to go
  - Tap a lead and it takes you there (a place in town, an app on the computer, the garage, under the bonnet…)
- **Leads: guided, but uncertain**
  - Every person, shop, video, forum post, manual and parcel can give you a lead
  - Each lead says **who said it** and **how sure they were**: "very sure" (the man at Motor Factors), "fairly sure" (a forum post from 2009), "maybe" (a text from Gaz), "usually wrong" (Gaz again)
  - How sure someone sounds is no guide: a confident lead can be a dead end, a doubtful one can be the answer
  - So you choose: the near, cheap, doubtful lead, or the far, dear, confident one
- **Never an endless, aimless search**
  - The game always keeps at least one lead that really moves things on, hidden among the others
  - Most leads are dead ends or loops, but each one crosses something off, so the notebook shows you what's left
- **When you're stuck (no leads)**
  - The job shows **No leads** in red, and you feel lost for a while; that's part of it
  - Something always turns up, but slowly:
    - The phone rings (Mum, Gaz, a shop calling back, a scam)
    - A parcel arrives, or a neighbour wanders over
    - You remember something ("didn't Gaz have one of these?")
    - **Sit in the car and think**: costs an hour, calms you down, gives a vague hunch ("an electrics place, maybe?")
- **Time, money and anger** pace it all
  - Time: each trip, call and wait uses game hours; shops open and shut; parcels take days
  - Money: parts, tools, fees; run low and some branches close
  - Anger: every snag raises it; too high and you kick something, which breaks it (a new job); tea and thinking bring it down. The angrier you are, the vaguer your hunches
- **A day in the game**
  - Morning: open the notebook, pick a lead
  - Go there (drive, call, look it up, get under the car)
  - Something happens: a tick, a dead end, a new branch, or a snag
  - The notebook updates; pick the next lead
  - Evening: shops shut; work in the garage or on the computer; parcels and calls arrive overnight
- **No end**: fixing the job reveals the next one (a different light comes on). Your score is how far you got: jobs ticked off, days survived, money spent, things kicked

Key:
- 📍 **Where** you do it:
  - **Home**: the front of the house (the parcel by the door)
  - **Computer**: ViewTube, Forum, Mail, Pics, MegaMart, eBuy, TechDocs
  - **Phone**: calls and texts, from any screen
  - **Garage**: the room (shelf of manuals, parts boxes, tool chest)
  - **Under the car**: the underside, and its zoom views
  - **Workbench**: the part and the hole
  - **City ›** a place on the map
- ✅ **Done**: this branch is finished (it hardly ever happens)
- ✖ **Dead end**: no way forward here; try another branch
- ↺ **Loops back** to a task you've already had
- ⏳ **Wait**: game time passes (days for the post, months for the mechanic)
- 😡 **Anger** goes up
- 💷 **Costs money**

## Snags that can strike any task

The game picks from these at random, so the same task goes differently each time. Each one opens new tasks or ends the branch.

- **Wrong part**: right box, wrong part; right part, wrong car; the picture showed a different one
- **Wrong size**: an 8 and a 10 but you need a 9; too long, too short, too thin, too deep
- **Sheared nut or bolt**: it snaps, leaving half a stud stuck in the hole
- **Broken tool**: the socket cracks, the ratchet slips, the extractor snaps inside the bolt
- **Can't get the tool in the gap**: the socket fits the nut, but there's no room to turn it; the long one doesn't fit, the short one doesn't reach
- **Password problem**: forgotten it, locked out after 3 tries, the reset code goes to an old email address
- **Phone dies**: in the middle of a call, while you're caller number 2, when you need the sign-in code
- **Computer crashes**: just as you press Buy; it won't start again → take it to 📍 City › PC Shop ⏳ 1 week
- **Closed**: half day, stocktaking, "back in 10 minutes" (2 hours), shut at 4:30 and you're there at 4:31
- **Out of business**: the shop is boarded up; the website still takes orders
- **On holiday**: the mechanic, the seller, the neighbour with your parcel, the only person who knows
- **Useless but close information**: the video, the manual or the forum post is for nearly the same car (a different year, engine, trim or country)
- **Baffling technical information**: true and complete, and no help at all (codes, references to other bulletins, missing diagrams)
- **Boxes of hundreds of screws**: the one you need is in there somewhere; tip them out and sort them ⏳, and it isn't there
- **Messy tools**: dozens of tools piled on the bench and in the chest stop you finding the one spanner you need ⏳ 😡
- **Lost nut**: it drops, bounces and rolls under the workbench; to reach it, clear everything off the floor ⏳
- **Bad or confusing advice from shops**: every counter gives a different answer, said with total confidence:
  - "You'll need the whole unit, they don't sell that bit"; "Just clean it with brake cleaner"; "Never use brake cleaner on that"
  - "That's a dealer job"; "Don't let the dealer near it"; "Ours is the same, just a different shape"
  - "Twenty years I've done this and I've never heard of that" (you read about it yesterday)
- **Out of stock**: "due in Thursday" (every Thursday); "one left" on the website, none on the shelf
- **Discontinued**: no longer made; the replacement part is also discontinued; the last one in the country was sold yesterday; "try a scrap yard"
- **Orders that take ages**: "2–3 working days" becomes 3 weeks; stuck at customs; the tracking says "in transit" for a fortnight; it goes to the depot, then back to the sender ⏳⏳
- **Misdelivered parcel**: "delivered" to a house 3 streets away, behind a bin, to a neighbour on holiday, or to the right street in another town; the photo proof shows someone else's door
- **Useless books on the shelf**: the manual for the diesel, the old model, the wrong language; the page you need is missing or stuck down with oil
