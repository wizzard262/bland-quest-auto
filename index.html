THE THANKLESS TASK Everyone's sure. Nobody's right.

HOW TO PLAY

  • The job: your car failed its test over a red light on the dash. Get it off.
  • Tap things in the picture (labels, boxes, buildings) or the buttons underneath.
  • The notebook (bottom right) lists your jobs. Each job has leads: things to try, who said them and how sure they were. Tap a lead to go there.
  • Leads can be wrong. Sure ones too. Dead ends get crossed out, so you can see what's left.
  • No leads? Sit in the car and think, ring someone, or wait: something always turns up.
  • Town: tap a place to go there (20 minutes). Drag the map to look round. Shops open and shut.
  • Home: computer (search, buy, read), garage, parcels by the front door, tea, bed.
  • Garage: under the car, under the bonnet, the driver's seat, the workbench, the toolbox.
  • Time, money and anger are at the top. Too angry and you kick something. It breaks.
  • The phone (bottom right) gets texts whenever it likes.
  • The game saves itself in this browser. New game starts again.
